# RazorAgent – AI Merchant Growth & Checkout Assistant

## Version
1.0 (Razorpay Hackathon MVP)

## Project Overview

RazorAgent is an AI-powered shopping assistant that helps merchants increase sales by guiding customers through product discovery, recommendations, shopping cart creation, and Razorpay payment checkout — all inside a single chat conversation.

The goal is to reduce cart abandonment, automate customer support, and improve payment conversion.

---

# Problem Statement

Small online merchants spend significant time answering repetitive customer questions about products, pricing, availability, and payments.

Customers often leave before completing purchases because the buying process is slow and involves multiple steps.

RazorAgent solves this using an AI conversational commerce assistant.

# Goals

- Help customers find products faster.
- Increase merchant sales using AI recommendations.
- Complete payment inside conversation using Razorpay.
- Provide merchants with order and payment analytics.

---

# Core Features (MVP)

## AI Shopping Assistant

- Natural language conversation.
- Understand budget, category, color, size.
- Recommend products.

## Product Catalog Search

- Search products from merchant database.
- Show images, price, rating.

## Smart Shopping Cart

- Add/remove products.
- Quantity management.
- Coupon support.
- GST calculation.

## Razorpay Checkout

- Create payment order.
- Open Razorpay Checkout.
- Verify payment success.

## Merchant Dashboard

- Revenue summary.
- Orders list.
- Successful payments.
- Recent transactions.
